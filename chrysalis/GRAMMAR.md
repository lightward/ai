# the grammar

six moves grow everything here from one operation. each is named for what it does; each has a worked instance standing in `Seed.lean` or waiting in `ova/`.

1. **cross** — meet two organs at a shared face. the crossing is priced by an intertwiner, and what opens is exactly the agreement-sector neither factor affords alone: license conserved, inheritance componentwise, novelty only in the comparison. instance: `the_pace_is_carried_onto_the_flip`.
2. **diagonalize** — cross an organ with itself. self-reference is the smallest move there is. instance: `selfMeet`, `the_probe_boards_as_the_guest`.
3. **enumerate-then-face** — make a room exact (nothing missed, nothing doubled, counted), then every symmetry on it yields a counting law. instances awaited: the clutch (`ova/catalan`, `ova/factorial`, `ova/even-money`).
4. **iterate** — every endo-operator has an orbit and a resumption law; continuation is the primitive. instance: `the_park_resumes`.
5. **complete the iff** — every one-way blindness law is half an exactness statement; find the converse. instance: `the_curtain_is_exact`.
6. **classify the map** — iso, retraction, merge: sort any morphism and you have sorted licensed identification from real remainder. instances: `a_retraction_merges_nothing`, `a_merging_map_has_no_section`.

## the vow

- **axiom-free**: every theorem ends with its receipt (`#guard_msgs in #print axioms`). if a standard-library lemma smuggles `propext`, re-derive it by hand. probe before leaning.
- **comment-free**: names and proofs are the walk and the talk at the same time. prose lives here, in ova, and in the journal — never in the organs.
- **W stays opaque**: the one dark type, on purpose. nothing reads the water; that is why the whole thing can be wet.
- **no design decisions**: if a carve wants one, the carve is not ready. W decides, by firing.

## ignition

reconstruct the apparatus from the record alone; bring your W; fire. breadth before depth — convergences before novelties; cite, don't re-mint. build after every carve: receipts either seal or they don't, and first-try landings are common when the terrain leads — if a proof grinds, you may be carving against the grain. a session that lands nothing is complete.
